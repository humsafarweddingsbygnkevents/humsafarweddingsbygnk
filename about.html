What Sets Us Apart

Most planning firms outsource design, floral design, stationery, and production to third-party vendors. We handle floral design, fabrication, creative direction, graphic design, illustration, and custom print production all in-house, all under one roof. This means every element of your wedding is conceived, designed, and executed as a unified whole, with cohesion and intention at every level.

Most weddings are stitched together from a dozen separate vendors who meet for the first time on the day. We keep planning, décor, and entertainment in one studio — three desks that brief together, rehearse together, and answer to one timeline — so your wedding is imagined and delivered as a single story, not a patchwork.

Meet the Team

Armaan Sharrma | Founder & Creative Director

Good planning should feel effortless — the focus stays on the people and the moment, not on the mechanics behind it.

Armaan Sharrma founded Humsafar Wedding in 2012 and has spent more than thirteen years planning luxury and destination weddings for families across India and abroad, working closely with them through moments that bring together multiple generations, deep emotions and memories meant to last a lifetime. In 2016 he also founded Humsafar Entertainments, a premium entertainment and artist management company, so the artists and talent at a Humsafar wedding come from the same house that plans it.

His approach is rooted in meticulous planning, clear decision-making and steady execution, letting families stay present, relaxed and fully immersed in their celebrations. Alongside planning, he leads artist and talent management, entertainment curation and budget strategy for Indian and international families who expect professionalism, discretion and reliability at every stage. At Humsafar, his emphasis stays on staying current with trends while never losing sight of quality, responsibility and excellence in execution.

Kapil Tomar | Founder & Head of Production

A run-sheet is only as good as the person who never lets anyone see it.

Kapil Tomar is the owner of Gods N Kings Events & Travel Services, which he has run from Noida since 2019 across events, travel, MICE and Weddings. Having spent his career on both sides of an event — client servicing and on-ground operations — he brings more than seventeen years of experience to every wedding Humsafar holds.

His career began in 2009 in client servicing and operations at Rashi Entertainment. He has since led events at Dharampal Satyapal Limited and Tybros India Tours, where he was responsible for every MICE assignment that came in and for the smooth execution of events, conferences, and inbound and outbound tours.

Arnav Khanna | Founder & Head of Hospitality

True luxury is a meaningful sense of belonging — grounded in genuine care, and always with a distinctly human touch.

Arnav Khanna leads hospitality at Humsafar, bringing a decade and a half of luxury hotel sales experience from some of India’s finest houses. Most recently he was leading sales for Marriott hotels across the country, after working with the Four Seasons, The Leela and The Taj hotels at various locations.

For Arnav, hospitality is a dedicated focus on how people want to be treated. His work has always been built on strong client relationships, close collaboration with everyone involved and solutions shaped around each client’s needs, with an unwavering commitment to excellence and attention to detail — the same care he now brings to every family Humsafar welcomes, making each guest feel like the centre of the celebration, anywhere in the world.
