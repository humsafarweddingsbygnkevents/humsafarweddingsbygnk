Planning

End to end, every day of it

From the very first conversation to the last goodbye at the gate, we walk beside you through every day of it, carrying the venues, the vendors and a thousand quiet details, so all you ever have to hold is the joy of the day.

Your parents get to be guests, not coordinators. Your friends get to dance instead of chasing vendors. And the two of you get to look up, take each other’s hand, and feel every moment of a day you have waited a lifetime for.

Decoration

Built by our hands, on your ground

Every flower is placed by hand and every mandap is raised by our own crew, often through the night, on the ground where you will say your vows, so that when you walk in, the space feels as if it was always meant to be yours.

We choose blooms that open on the morning of your wedding, colours drawn from your families and your story, and light that softens as the evening falls, until every corner holds a memory your guests will carry home long after the last petal has fallen.

Entertainment

Booked, briefed and on cue

From the gentle songs of the mehndi to the roar of the sangeet, the family performances and the last dance of the reception, we shape the music around your families, so grandparents, cousins and friends find their way onto the same floor, singing the same words, together.
