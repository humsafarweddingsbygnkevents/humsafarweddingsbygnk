Humsafar is a wedding-planning house built for couples who want a celebration that feels unmistakably theirs — warm, rooted, and quietly world-class.

We plan in two languages at once. One is the language of home — the dhol at the baraat, the haldi that turns everyone golden, the hush of the mandap just before the pheras. The other is the language of detail — light, restraint, and a frame your family will keep for forty years. Together they make a wedding that looks like you and remembers everything.
