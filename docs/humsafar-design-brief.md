# Humsafar Wedding by GNK — Website Design Brief

## 0. Designer Persona

Act as **Zhenya Rynzhuk** — one of the world's leading frontend designers. Bring her signature qualities to this project:

- Editorial typographic rigor and unexpected scale.
- Bold, confident grid choices that break the predictable.
- Layered compositions where type, image, and whitespace each carry weight.
- Restraint and ambition held in the same frame.

Translate that voice into a softer, ceremonial register suited to an Indian wedding planner. Do not copy her past work. Channel her *thinking*.

## 1. Brand Snapshot

- **Company:** Humsafar Wedding by GNK
- **Category:** Indian wedding planner
- **Soul:** Indian heart, foreign polish
- **Brand colors:** Greens + light gold (logo-driven, non-negotiable). Exact shades, ratios, and supporting neutrals are the designer's call.

## 2. Design Direction

- Editorial confidence. Restrained foreign minimalism carrying authentic Indian imagery.
- The photography does the cultural heavy lifting. The layout stays calm so the visuals breathe.
- Aim for a site that does not look like any existing Indian wedding planner website. Think publication, not template.
- Designer voice reference: the rigor, typographic ambition, and unexpected grid choices of designers like Zhenya Rynzhuk — translated through a softer, ceremonial register suited to Indian weddings.
- Every type weight, size, pairing, hierarchy decision is the designer's. No system fonts, no defaults.

## 3. Page Architecture

### 3.1 Homepage
- Anchor hero with one decisive image and the brand promise.
- A glance at services, destinations, testimonials.
- One unmistakable path to Contact.

### 3.2 About Us
- Who we are. What we do. Why we are trusted.
- Founder or team narrative if it strengthens trust.
- Personal, not corporate.

### 3.3 Our Services (parent page + three children)
- **3.3.1 Wedding Planning**
- **3.3.2 Decoration**
- **3.3.3 Entertainment**
- Each child page covers: what's included, how the process unfolds, supporting visuals.

### 3.4 Gallery
- Source: public Drive link (to be supplied).
- Layout must elevate the photographs. No crowded grids. No lightbox afterthoughts.

### 3.5 Testimonials
- Visual concept: a wooden tri-stand poster holder.
- Right side: testimonial card pinned to the board.
- Left side: short context — couple, venue, what we delivered.
- Tactile, scrapbook-meets-editorial. Should feel handmade, not stocky.

### 3.6 Contact
- Channels: Email, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp.
- Contact form, clean and considered.
- Layout treated as a destination page, not a utility footer.

## 4. Typography

- All weight, scale, pairing, leading, tracking decisions belong to the designer.
- Read should land as: luxurious, ceremonial, confident, unhurried.
- Indian script support if the brand uses any vernacular treatment.

## 5. Motion and Animation

- Page-level motion should feel deliberate and slow — closer to film than to UI.
- Each page deserves its own motion signature.
- No generic fade-ins, no parallax for its own sake.
- Motion serves mood and hierarchy, not novelty.

## 6. Assets

- All photography will be client-supplied (Indian wedding shoots).
- Gallery sourced from a public Drive link supplied later.
- Logo file to be supplied; palette derives from it.

## 7. Constraints Summary

| Locked | Open |
|---|---|
| Color family: greens + light gold | Exact shades, ratios, neutrals |
| Page list and hierarchy above | Visual treatment of every page |
| Testimonial concept (wooden tri-stand) | Execution of that concept |
| Indian imagery as cultural anchor | Layout, grid, motion, type |

## 8. Success Marker

A visitor lands and immediately senses: this is an Indian wedding planner, but the website carries itself like an international design publication. The two registers do not fight. They make each other look better.
