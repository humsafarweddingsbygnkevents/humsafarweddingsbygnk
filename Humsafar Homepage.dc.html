01
An Indian heart, a foreign polish

We plan in two languages. They agree.

The first language is ours — saffron, agarbatti, the particular hush of a mandap minutes before the bride arrives. The second is the one your photographer's editor speaks in Paris. We plan so the two never have to compete.

Seven years, two hundred and fourteen couples, nineteen cities. A studio of nine. A roster of vendors we trust without checking. And a habit of arriving early.
