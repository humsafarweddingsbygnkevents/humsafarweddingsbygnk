A wooden tri-stand sits at the studio. After every wedding, a couple sends a note. We read it once, out loud, and then we pin it.

Aanya & Ranveer
The Leela, Udaipur · February 2024

A four-day affair on the lake. We delivered the mandap by boat, kept two wardrobes in continuous rotation, and timed the baraat to the call of the muezzin from the city across the water.

"

They held our wedding the way you'd hold a sleeping child. Carefully. And without telling anyone how tired their arms were.

— Aanya, the bride
Feb '24
"

We hired three companies and got one family. By the sangeet, nobody could tell where our relatives ended and the Humsafar crew began.

— Aditya, the groom
Mar '24
Bhumika & Aditya
Rambagh Palace, Jaipur · March 2024

Three days, six hundred guests, one colour story. We built a marigold mandap overnight, ran a haldi at dawn, and had the courtyard cleared and reset for the pheras by sundown.

Meher & Zoya
A farmhouse, Chhatarpur · December 2023

An intimate winter wedding under string lights. We kept it small, warm and unhurried — a brass band at dusk, a bonfire, and a vidaai that nobody wanted to start.

"

We asked for small and they gave us small done perfectly — which, it turns out, is the hardest thing of all.

— Meher
Dec '23
